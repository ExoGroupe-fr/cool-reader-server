The first version of Cool Reader Server: a home server for comics, manga and ebooks, in the spirit of Plex.

- **Libraries** from your folders, with series recognised and matched on free online catalogues: posters, synopses,
  authors, genres.
- **Members** with the libraries you choose, invited by QR code or link, each with their own reading progress.
- **In the browser**: what you're reading, what's new, search, and a reader for comics, manga and PDFs.
- **HTTPS** with a certificate the server makes itself, **Bonjour** on the home network, and **remote access** that
  opens the router's port (UPnP, NAT-PMP), with an optional free DuckDNS name.
- **OPDS 1.2** with page streaming, for other readers.
- **Cool Reader** (iPhone, iPad) connects to it from its version 1.2.

**Install**
- **Docker** (NAS, Linux, Raspberry Pi): `ghcr.io/exogroupe-fr/cool-reader-server:0.1.0`, see
  [deploy/compose.yaml](https://github.com/ExoGroupe-fr/cool-reader-server/blob/main/deploy/compose.yaml).
- **Mac** (macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel): download `CoolReaderServer-0.1.0-mac-universal.zip` below
  and move the app to Applications. It is signed and notarized by Apple.
